Pentra Forge

Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

In short: your training data is yours. It lives on your phone and syncs to our cloud only when you sign in. We do not sell data, show ads or use analytics trackers. You can delete your training data at any time in Settings.

Who we are

Pentra Forge is run by Pentra (“we”, “us”). Questions about your data: pentrascans@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Usage signals (only with an account): when you first get ranked, unlock a skill or start a plan, and how fast the app opened — the event type and one number, never your workout content. We use them to check that features work. They are deleted with your account.
  • Account. Your email address and a password, handled by our sign-in provider. Your Pentra account is shared with other Pentra apps.
  • Training data you enter. Workouts, sets, exercises, coach notes, programs and goals, body measurements, recovery and nutrition logs, and settings. Without an account this stays on your phone only. When you sign in, it is synced to our cloud database so it is backed up and available on your other devices.
  • Progress photos. Stored only on your phone, unless you switch on photo backup; then they are uploaded to private storage that only your account can open.
  • Leaderboard (optional). If you join, your display name, optional country and the ratings we calculate from your synced workouts. Other users see your display name, country, rating and rank — never your bodyweight or sex.
  • Payments. The plan you chose, the reference number and note you submit, and your subscription dates. You pay with a QR code in your own banking or e-wallet app; we never receive your card or bank details.
  • On your device. The app stores your data and an offline copy of itself in your browser so it works without signal. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies or trackers.

Why we use it

  • To run the app, sync your devices and back up your training.
  • To calculate ranks and leaderboards you choose to join.
  • To confirm payments and manage your subscription.
  • To answer your support requests and keep the service secure.

Who processes it for us

We use Supabase for sign-in, our database and photo storage, and a web host to serve the app. They process data only to provide these services to us. Their servers may be outside the Philippines. We do not sell or rent your data, and we share it with others only when the law requires.

How long we keep it

Your training data stays until you delete it. Payment records are kept as long as needed for accounting and legal duties, even after you delete your training data.

Deleting your data

  • Settings → Delete my training data removes your synced training records, your leaderboard entry and any backed-up photos from our cloud, and can also erase your phone’s copy.
  • Leaving the leaderboard deletes your entry, scores and friend list.
  • To close your Pentra account itself, email us.

Your rights

Under the Philippines’ Data Privacy Act of 2012 you can ask to access, correct, download or delete your personal data, object to its processing, and complain to the National Privacy Commission. Email us and we will respond.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections. Our database rules let each account read and change only its own records, and leaderboard scores are calculated on our server.

Children

If you are under 18, please use Pentra Forge with permission from a parent or guardian.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.

Automatic problem reports

When the app hits an error on a signed-in tester’s phone, it sends us the technical details only — the error message, a short technical trace, the screen you were on, the app version and your browser type — so we can fix it. It never includes your workouts, measurements or photos. Reports are grouped and kept until the problem is fixed.
