What’s your Forge Rank?

Enter one set — a gym lift, or pull-ups, dips and push-ups for the Calisthenics Rank. It’s calculated exactly like the app does it — on your phone, nothing is sent anywhere. Sign up and the app picks up your gym set for you.

Enter a set to see your rank.

How accurate is it?

  • Estimated 1RM: the Epley formula, weight × (1 + reps ÷ 30). A single counts as your 1RM.
  • Best range: sets of about 2–10 reps give the most reliable estimate; the app ranks sets up to 12 reps and flags higher ones.
  • Fair for bodyweight: DOTS, the formula powerlifting uses to compare lifters of different sizes, with separate curves for men and women.
  • An estimate, not a max test: watch your trend over weeks rather than one number. Leaderboard ratings are recalculated on our server from synced workouts.

Every set counts

Your estimated one-rep max from any set of 12 reps or fewer — so a hard triple can rank you up, not just a max attempt.

Fair at any bodyweight

A 60 kg lifter and a 100 kg lifter are rated on the same scale, with separate curves for men and women.

Verified, not typed in

Leaderboard ratings are calculated on our server from your synced workouts. Nobody can paste in a fake number.

What lifters say

Questions

Good to know

Is Pentra Forge free?

Every feature is free for 30 days. After that it is ₱129 a month or ₱1,199 a year. If you do not renew, you can still open the app to view and export everything you logged.

How is my rank calculated?

For each ranked lift — squat, bench press, deadlift, Romanian deadlift, overhead press, incline bench and barbell row — we estimate your one-rep max from sets of 12 reps or fewer, adjust it for your bodyweight with DOTS, and turn it into a rating from 1 to 1,000. Your overall rank is the average of your ranked lifts.

Do I need an account?

No. You can use Pentra Forge on one phone without an account. An account syncs your phones, backs up your training and lets you join the leaderboard. Your Pentra account works here too.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once opened, the app works without internet. Workouts save on your phone and sync the next time you are online.

Which phones does it work on?

Any modern browser on Android or iPhone, and on computers. On Android tap “Install app”. On iPhone, open it in Safari, tap Share, then “Add to Home Screen”.

Can I bring my workout history?

Yes. Import a CSV export from Hevy or Strong, or paste workouts as text with Quick log.

Who can see my data?

Only you. The leaderboard is opt-in and shows your display name, optional country, rating and rank — never your bodyweight or sex. Progress photos stay on your phone unless you turn on backup.

What are the ten Forge Ranks?

From Rank 1 to Rank 10: Unforged, Ironclad, Vanguard, Beastmode, Juggernaut, Titan, Colossus, Mythic, Forge Master and Greek God. Each rank has five divisions (V up to I), so there is always a next step. Try the calculator above to see where one of your sets lands.

Do you charge me automatically?

No. There is no card on file and nothing renews by itself. When you want Premium, you pay by QR from your banking or e-wallet app and enter the reference number; we confirm it, usually within a day, and you get a notification.

Can I train with friends or coach someone?

Yes. Add friends to a friends-only leaderboard, send a training plan to one friend or several at once, and invite people with your link — when invited friends start training, you earn extra Premium days.

How accurate is the rank estimate?

It uses the Epley formula and DOTS, the same tools coaches and powerlifting use. Estimates are most reliable from sets of about 2–10 reps, and they are estimates, not a max test — so watch your trend over weeks. See “How accurate is it?” in the ranks section for the details.

Can I use pounds, light mode or bigger text?

Yes. Switch between kg and lb any time, choose a light or dark theme, and the app works with your phone’s larger text and reduced-motion settings.
